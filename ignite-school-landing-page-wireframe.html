A real American school, built for life in the UAE

Ignite follows the New York State learning standards and gives space to Emirati culture, Arabic and Islamic values. Children learn by doing, asking questions and setting goals.

  • Hands-on, student-centred and inquiry-based lessons
  • Individual learning plans, goals and learning portfolios for each child
  • Skills for the future: critical thinking, teamwork, communication and creativity
  • Arabic language and Islamic education alongside the American programme

Good to know

Which grades does Ignite teach?

Ignite teaches Pre-Kindergarten to Grade 12, with an Early Learning Center, Elementary, Middle and High School.

Which curriculum do you follow?

An American curriculum based on the New York State P-12 Common Core Learning Standards, adapted to life and culture in the UAE.

Where is the school and what are the school hours?

Ignite is on Street 414 in Al Warqa’a 3, Dubai. School runs Monday to Thursday from 7:30 am to 4:30 pm and Friday from 7:30 am to 2:00 pm.

What are the fees?

Fees depend on the grade. Request the fee schedule and our team will send it, along with the payment methods. [Add fee range once confirmed]

Is there bus transport?

Yes, a bus service is available. Ask the admissions team whether it covers your area. [Confirm wording with school]

Do you support children who need extra help?

Yes. The campus has English language centres and learning support centres, and the school promotes inclusive education.
